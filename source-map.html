Reading the source conventions

The 2026 course URL, IAP label, and linked filenames identify the selected edition. The slide covers also print January 2025 dates; that inconsistency is retained as a source limitation rather than treated as verification of exact delivery dates.

The recording order takes precedence when it differs from the written notes. In particular, score matching precedes stochastic sampling in Lecture 3-A, and latent representations precede architectures in Lecture 4. Chapter authors read their complete assigned caption text and checked technical claims against written sources; this does not establish full visual or audiovisual review.

Noise schedules, finite-risk hypotheses, and endpoint limits are stated beside the relevant results. The book derives Gaussian log scores and distinguishes them from density gradients. The discrete chapter explicitly translates the source's column-generator convention into its declared row convention. These qualifications are part of the mathematical exposition, rather than assumptions inferred from abbreviated source statements.
